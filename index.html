Click a flow to list its energies; click an energy for example ion paths and plots.

The energy cuts in the tables are not thermal energies. “Extracted < 0.1 eV” and “Depth to 1 eV” use the ion's kinetic energy in the lab frame. A thermal ion has about (3/2)kT in the frame of the local gas, about 3 meV in 23 K neon and 0.5 meV in 4 K helium, so these columns count ions that have slowed down, not ions in equilibrium with the gas.
